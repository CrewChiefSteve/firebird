Why this car

The story

A few years ago this Firebird came apart on Steve's shop floor. Every panel off, every bracket bagged, the shell shot in red oxide primer and set aside. Life and racing got in the way, the way they do.

Then Jennifer Jo Cobb asked a question: could her dad drive it on his birthday? Joe Cobb has spent a lifetime around race cars, and Jennifer wanted to hand him the keys to this one on February 14, 2027.

So the car is back on the bench. Steve and Nick are working it in phases, on the clock, and posting the whole thing here as it happens. The first goal is a rolling chassis in two weeks. The last goal is Joe behind the wheel.
